## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a forward-looking union between high-level biotechnology and warm, organic ecology. It avoids the clinical coldness of pure scientific tech while steering clear of overly rustic or muted earthen cliches. The aesthetic is clean, luxurious, and rooted in nature—evoking vitality, research precision, and sustainable living.

### Visual Pillars
- **Organic Warmth & Grounded Tactility:** Layered backgrounds transition between warm oat tones, soft sunlit limestone, and deep botanical underpinnings rather than stark sterile white.
- **Biotech Vitality:** High-contrast forest emeralds combined with vibrant sprout greens deliver immediate energy and forward-thinking sustainability cues.
- **Sculpted Precision:** Generously rounded planar modules, pill-shaped controls, and floating contextual panels provide an effortless, refined structure that frames both scientific data and natural imagery with clarity.

## Colors

The color palette draws directly from lush botanical foliage, sunlit moss, and warm architectural wood tones. It achieves high readability and structural separation through rich organic contrasts rather than faded tints.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary Forest Green (`#2E5C38`):** The foundational anchor for major call-to-actions, prominent navigation caps, deep headers, and high-emphasis active tokens. Provides structural authority and deep botanical presence.
- **Secondary Meadow Moss (`#82AC42`):** Used for interactive focus, badges, accent highlights, verification nodes, and progressive metrics. Infuses vibrant life and biotech vigor into surfaces.
- **Tertiary Warm Timber (`#7B4B28`):** Represents grounded warmth, architectural details, secondary pill tags, and textural contrasts. Prevents the green spectrum from feeling monolithic.
- **Neutral Natural Surface (`#F7F6EE`):** A warm, unbleached oat-milk canvas that replaces sterile digital whites, giving ambient light and organic calm to page layouts.
- **Deep Botanical Ink (`#152418`):** Ultra-dark evergreen charcoal for primary typographic readability, preserving contrast ratios (WCAG AAA) across warm parchment layers.
- **Container Tiers (`#EEEDE0` to `#E3E1CE`):** Slightly stepped warm-neutral slabs providing distinct module separation without relying on abrasive borders.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the structured, editorial weight of **Epilogue** with the modern, fluid readability of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Display & Headings:** Epilogue brings architectural confidence and clean, structural geometry. In display roles, its tighter letter-spacing evokes modern sustainability journalism and advanced life-science publications.
- **Body & Controls:** Plus Jakarta Sans features rounded apertures and open counters that remain legible over varied textured photography and tinted organic surfaces.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Rendered with elevated weights (`600`–`700`) and slight tracking to reinforce clarity inside dense biotech summaries, floating badges, and pill buttons.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a structured 12-column grid system resting inside a centered max container width of `76rem` (`1216px`). 

- **Spatial Rhythm:** Generous vertical rhythm utilizing `space-3xl` (`4rem`) and `space-4xl` (`6rem`) between major content blocks conveys architectural space and high-end living.
- **Overlapping Card Composition:** Content modules float over background imagery and tinted tier blocks with controlled vertical offsets (`-1.5rem` to `-2.5rem`), mirroring the floating search consoles and featured story cards seen in modern landscape-driven interfaces.
- **Adaptive Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `gutter-mobile` (`1rem`), single-column stacks with edge-to-edge floating action drawers.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, 2-column card layouts.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns, `gutter-desktop` (`2rem`), multi-layer stacked sections with asymmetric editorial pacing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses sculpted tonal layering and diffused botanical shadows rather than heavy borders or generic grays.

- **Warm Surface Tiers:** Content layers stack using tonal changes:
  - `Base`: Background canvas `#F7F6EE`
  - `Layer 1`: Crisp Floating Cards `#FFFFFF` or Light Tint `#F0EFE5`
  - `Layer 2`: Sub-components, Search Trays, and Chip groups `#E7E5D6`
- **Tonal Organic Shadows:** Shadows are warm and diffused, tinted with deep moss and umber tones rather than black:
  - *Floating Trays & Bars:* `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(46, 92, 56, 0.12), 0 4px 12px rgba(123, 75, 40, 0.05)`
  - *Interactive Hover Elevation:* `0 20px 40px -10px rgba(46, 92, 56, 0.18)`
- **Atmospheric Glass Overlays:** For elements overlaid directly on landscape photographs (such as top navigation bars and hero search inputs), apply a 12px frosted blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with `rgba(247, 246, 238, 0.85)` fill, creating tactile integration between nature imagery and interactive controls.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a distinctive pill-shaped and organic pebble geometry. 

- **Pill Philosophy (`roundedness: 3`):** Primary action buttons, floating query toolbars, badge tags, and search bars use continuous full-pill curvature (`9999px`), giving them a soft, ergonomic quality.
- **Card Containers:** Medium to large content wrappers utilize sweeping `2rem` (`rounded-lg`) to `3rem` (`rounded-xl`) corner radii.
- **Media Cutouts:** Imagery and photo frames feature consistent rounded corners matching the container nesting hierarchy, avoiding harsh geometric clashes with surrounding modules.

## Components

### Buttons & Actions
- **Primary Button:** Filled `#2E5C38` with crisp white or warm oat text, completely pill-rounded (`9999px`), inner padding `0.875rem 1.75rem`. On hover, subtle elevation lift and slight darkening to `#24492C`.
- **Secondary Button:** Surface filled with vibrant sprout green `#82AC42` and contrasting dark botanical text (`#152418`) for high-conversion actions.
- **Ghost / Outlined Button:** Transparent fill with `1.5px` border in `#2E5C38` or subtle warm taupe, retaining pill silhouette.

### Floating Search & Filter Bar
- A unified pill-shaped console floating across boundary sections.
- Divided into logical segment blocks separated by delicate warm vertical dividers (`#DCD9C7`).
- Features inline circular action buttons (`#2E5C38` or `#82AC42`) for immediate search submission.

### Cards & Group Containers
- Built on bright white (`#FFFFFF`) or soft limestone (`#F0EFE5`) surfaces with generous internal padding (`1.75rem` to `2.25rem`).
- Rounded corners (`2rem` radius) with top-anchored or inset imagery featuring rounded borders.
- Floating metric chips embedded on the top right or bottom edge of image banners.

### Chips, Tags & Badges
- Compact pill-shaped micro-containers (`roundedness: 3`).
- **Eco-Status Tags:** `#E4ECC8` background paired with `#2E5C38` typography.
- **Timber Detail Tags:** Subtle warm earth fill `#F0E5DC` paired with `#7B4B28` text.

### Form Inputs
- Enclosed pill or heavy-rounded (`1.25rem`) fields with `#FFFFFF` background, subtle inset border `#DDD9C8`, and focused ring in `#82AC42` (`2px`).
- Placeholder copy tinted in muted forest gray (`#6E7A6E`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Rounded squircle checkboxes (`0.375rem`) and circular radios finished with `#2E5C38` fill when active, featuring clean white glyphs.
